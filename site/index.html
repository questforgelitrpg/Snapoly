Progress, privacy & prototype notes

Campaign progress, coins, tool inventory and MagnetFlow saves live in your browser or installed Android app. There is no account or cloud save. Different browsers and the Android app have separate saves; clearing app or browser data removes them. A saved MagnetFlow run can be resumed, while starting a new MagnetFlow run replaces it. The game includes no analytics or advertising code. The website host may keep standard request logs. Feedback you email is sent through your email provider.

The Android APK is a signed prototype for direct testing. The iPhone version is an installable web app, rather than an IPA or App Store release. Music and effects can be muted separately; background music starts at 20% or less.
