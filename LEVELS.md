# Snapoly 0.5.1 progression

All five shapes follow this path. Non-Cube shapes retain two additional seconds in the starting travel baseline, before speed multipliers. Levels 1–4 remain tool-free and add sides gradually, with tighter arrival spacing than 0.5.0. Level 5 introduces Slow Time, more cells and the first speed rise. Each level from 6 onward changes one category. Speed rises affect both travel time and arrival spacing; clocks still pause during clear/settle effects.

Travel is the opening deadline; later cells in each campaign run have up to 8% less travel time. Arrival spacing applies when an incoming slot is available, with a maximum of four. Speed steps are +15%, +10%, +15%, +25%, +25%, +25% at levels 5, 7, 12, 16, 18 and 20 respectively.

The former Cube Master opening travel was 13.91 seconds. It is now approximately level 12's 13.75 seconds, between Novice (10 wins) and Expert (15 wins). All six colors are introduced by Expert: Violet at 11, Sapphire at 13 and Lime at 15. The Master finish has 60 cells, six colors, 7.04-second opening travel and 0.70-second arrivals. Completing it remains the Master badge requirement; no mandatory tool spending or retroactive badge revocation was added.

Every level has an illustrated briefing and Play gate, including every speed rise. Beginner / Novice / Expert / Master require 5 / 10 / 15 / 20 distinct wins in a shape. Existing records, badges, coins and stock are retained. Next campaign shape access still opens after the preceding shape's fourth level.

## Lives, tools and continuous modes

From campaign level 10 onward, every three match clears earns one spare, up to five. The former two-clear earning bonus at level 15 is replaced by the full-palette introduction. Existing pool and partial progress persist; at zero active lives a spare restores one automatically. Early levels do not earn or spend spares.

Endless starts at 16-second travel / 1.8-second arrivals with three colors, three lives, tools and run-only earned spares. MagnetFlow starts at 14-second travel / 1.6-second arrivals with all six colors, one life, and no earned spares. A compatible face held within 20 degrees catches after 0.09 seconds, then pulls over 0.32 seconds. Six-color snapshots save and resume correctly. Existing three-color snapshots remain valid: their current cells, timers, inventory and score are retained; new incoming cells use the new six-color settings.

Every continuous mode still compounds a random 3–10% speed rise every 25 successful placements; misses and Bomb removals do not count. MagnetFlow pauses every 100 placements for Keep going, Save & exit, or End run. Growth Lab still retains 100 cubes with no clears or life limit.

Cube is always available in Endless. Other Flow shapes require their own Beginner badge; MagnetFlow also needs any one Novice badge. Tools, research costs, two-slot loadouts and coin rewards are unchanged. See BALANCE.md and DESIGN_GUIDE.md.
