All five shapes follow this path. All non-Cube shapes start with two extra seconds of travel; later speed multipliers apply to that baseline. Level 4 still opens the next shape. Level 5 deliberately combines the first tool with a small speed increase and more cells; levels 6-20 each change one category.

Every level has a visual briefing and Play gate. Each shape awards Beginner / Novice / Expert / Master at 5 / 10 / 15 / 20 distinct wins. Replays improve records without increasing completed-level counts.

The spare pool holds five. Each match clear counts once, including full clears. At zero active lives, a spare restores one life automatically. Campaign pool and partial earning progress persist between levels and retries. Early levels do not earn or spend spares. Endless has a run-only pool. MagnetFlow has one life, no spares, a pause every 100 placements and a resumable save.

## Continuous rewards

Cube is always available in Endless Flow. A shape's Beginner badge (5 distinct wins in that shape) unlocks it in Endless and MagnetFlow. The first Novice badge (10 distinct wins in one shape) unlocks MagnetFlow itself. Replays and wins split between shapes do not bypass these gates. Campaign shape access at level 4 is unchanged.

MagnetFlow uses Endless clearing and tool drops with one life and no earned spares. All continuous modes speed up by a random 3–10% of their current speed every 25 successful placements. Instead of Snap, rotate to hold an unobstructed compatible face within 20 degrees of the selected incoming lane. After a short 0.09-second stable aim the magnet completes alignment and attachment over 0.32 seconds. Each flow mode stores a separate best score for each shape.

Slow and Bomb each grant two introduction charges once per profile. Existing one-charge grants gain only the extra charge once. Continuous runs start with two of each tool. Coins and prices are documented in BALANCE.md.

Research adds Shield (Novice, 140 coins; refill 65) and Recolor (Expert, 220; refill 80). Equip at most two. Owned research tools become available from campaign level 7 and in both Flow modes; earlier starters stay tool-free. See DESIGN_GUIDE.md for the consolidated current specification.
